Observations, decisions, and open questions as the enhancer research project develops.

11 October 2026Local observations

From collected files to trustworthy comparisons

The collection is present. The next task is to make sure that rows, constructs, and independent measurements mean what we think they mean.

The folder audit identified repeated measurements across the replicate and combined ENCODE TSVs, two control-name differences in the lentiMPRA design/results join, and exact overlap between the local legacy development and independent-test enhancer files.

What this changes

The legacy files need an independence audit before model evaluation. The lentiMPRA pipeline needs explicit source selection and construct mapping before activity comparisons.

The 55,977 construct names in the supplied specificity table can be recovered with a barcode-coverage rule. Reconstructing the numerical specificity scores remains unresolved. This is progress on data quality and reproduction, not a new biological discovery.

10 October 2026Research direction

Begin with enhancer activity and its context

The initial strategy brings together the biology, the collected publications, and a proposed comparison of matched sequences across three cellular contexts.

The first aim is an understandable, reproducible analysis. A later K562 cross-assay comparison is conditional on suitable independent measurements and enough overlapping tested loci. Synthetic motif interactions remain a mechanistic alternative.
