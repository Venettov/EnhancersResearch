Welcome to the project

How does the same DNA behave
in different cellular contexts?

Enhancers help control when and where genes are expressed. Their activity depends on sequence, cell state, and the surrounding regulatory environment.

EnhancersResearch brings together functional assays, genomic annotations, developmental experiments, and sequence models to explore that context. Our starting point is a careful comparison of measured activity in HepG2, K562, and WTC11 cells.
