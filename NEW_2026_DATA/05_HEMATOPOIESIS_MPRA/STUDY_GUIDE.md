# Synthetic enhancers in hematopoiesis

## What is here

- `data_updated2.rda`: processed MPRA data.
- `PAPERS/`: the 2024 bioRxiv preprint PDF and the 2025 peer-reviewed Cell article as full-text HTML.
- `SUPPLEMENTARY/`: Cell Tables S1–S4 (library design, antibodies, oligonucleotide sequences, processing parameters).
- `CODE/`: source snapshots for the figure, processing, and prediction repositories.

## Recommended reading

Use the published Cell article as the primary reference; the preprint is included to preserve the version that accompanied the original data discovery. The work tests synthetic sequences across seven hematopoietic progenitor states and K562, which makes it especially relevant for cell-state-specific enhancer prediction. Follow the paper’s variable definitions when selecting processed values.
