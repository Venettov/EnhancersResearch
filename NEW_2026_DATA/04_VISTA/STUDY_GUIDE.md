# VISTA Enhancer Browser

## What is here

The folder contains the official VISTA database tables, coordinate BED files for hg38 and mm10, dictionaries, and field descriptions. `PAPERS/` has a local open full-text HTML copy of the updated database paper. Use a browser to open the HTML file; the journal and PMC links are below.

## Recommended reading

This resource is useful as an in-vivo developmental validation set. Its assay tests candidate sequences in transgenic mouse embryos, so its positive and negative results have a different scope from cell-culture MPRA/STARR measurements. Check species, developmental stage, and genome assembly before comparing records.

## Citation and links
