# ENCODE4 / SCREEN candidate regulatory elements

## What is here

- `GRCh38-cCREs.ELS.bed`: the downloaded enhancer-like candidate cis-regulatory elements (pELS and dELS) from the ENCODE Registry V4 release.
- `PAPERS/`: the 2026 Nature paper, *An expanded registry of candidate cis-regulatory elements*.
- `SUPPLEMENTARY/`: supplementary methods/figures, reporting summary, tables 1–17, supplementary data 1–6, and peer-review file.

## Recommended reading

Start with `PAPERS/An_expanded_registry_of_candidate_cis-regulatory_elements_Nature_2026.pdf`, then the supplementary information. The BED file is a candidate annotation: it is useful for defining or stratifying regions, but by itself does not prove enhancer activity in a given cell type.

## Citation and links

Moore JE et al. “An expanded registry of candidate cis-regulatory elements.” *Nature* (2026). DOI: [10.1038/s41586-025-09909-9](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-025-09909-9).

The downloaded BED comes from [SCREEN Registry V4](https://downloads.wenglab.org/Registry-V4/GRCh38-cCREs.ELS.bed). The paper’s supplemental data are also hosted by the [Moore Lab](https://users.moore-lab.org/ENCODE-cCREs/Supplementary-Data/).
