How to read this comparison

The model asks

Does this 200-base sequence resemble enhancers in the historical benchmark? The frozen SVM produces a decision score. It is not a probability, and cell identity is not an input.

The experiment asks

How much reporter RNA is produced relative to reporter DNA in this cell? Larger log2(RNA/DNA) values indicate higher relative reporter activity. Zero means a normalized ratio of one; it is not an active/inactive cutoff.

The demo asks

How do the rankings relate in this already examined sample? A relationship can motivate a fresh test. It does not establish biological accuracy, target genes, or activity in every cell type.

Data, matching, and coverage
  • Inputs: the original 200 server-comparison probes: 185 HepG2 candidate enhancers and 15 promoters from Table S10. Their prior selection required hg19 annotations and screening against known historical sequences; this is a selected sample of the joint library.
  • Exact matching: the 200-base insert in each S10 construct matches the original FASTA and the saved prediction’s sequence hash. Constructs are joined to S11 by exact name, without combining opposite orientations or applying control-name aliases.
  • Published measurements: means are taken directly from S11’s all_cell_types_summary, at its published precision. They were checked against the per-cell summaries and the average of available replicate log2 values, allowing for rounding.
  • Plot eligibility: a reported mean in every cell and at least one replicate with ≥10 observed barcodes in each cell. This is the coverage rule that recovers the published specificity-table membership in the earlier audit; we do not compute or validate specificity scores here.
  • Scores: saved Layer I predictions from the unchanged October 2026 model. Spearman correlations use average ranks for ties and the same eligible records in each cell. Layer II’s historical strong/weak labels are not treated as experimental high/low activity.

What remains for a new result: a separate analysis protocol, verified fresh sequences and their relationship to training data and earlier probes, appropriate activity processing, and an uncertainty analysis. The original independent-evaluation plan remains in place. This demo does not resolve the outstanding element-specificity-score normalization question.
